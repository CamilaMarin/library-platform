# Product — EntreLíneas

EntreLíneas is a web platform for managing personal libraries (physical and digital) and encouraging shared reading among families and small book clubs within a family group. It is not a public social network for readers, nor a shared file repository.

**Purpose:** stories bring people together; the app only facilitates that.

**Target users:** reading families (primary), small book clubs within a family group, individual readers within a group. NOT for casual public readers, nor for researchers with academic bibliographic needs.

**Product principles (non-negotiable):**
1. People come before books.
2. The user always owns their library and their data.
3. Privacy by Design (Chile's Ley 21.719, from the first sprint).
4. The app supports reading, it doesn't replace it — no distracting social-network mechanics.
5. Everything should feel simple, even complex functionality.
6. Cloud-agnostic and zero-cost by default.
7. Mobile-first.
